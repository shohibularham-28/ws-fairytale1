LANGUAGE FOCUS

Simple Past Tense

Narrative texts always use Simple Past Tense. Study the three forms below together with your group.
Narrative text selalu menggunakan Simple Past Tense. Pelajari dulu ketiga bentuknya di bawah ini bersama kelompokmu.

🧩 Simple Past Tense Formula

FormFormulaExample
(+) PositiveS + Verb 2Cinderella cleaned the house.
(–) NegativeS + did not (didn't) + Verb 1She did not go to the party at first.
(?) InterrogativeDid + S + Verb 1 + ?Did Cinderella lose her shoe?
Nominal (to be)S + was/were + ...The prince was very handsome.
BUILDING KNOWLEDGE OF THE FIELD

Text 1 — Cinderella

Read the following text carefully with your group, then discuss and complete the tasks below on paper.
Bacalah teks berikut dengan saksama bersama kelompokmu, lalu diskusikan dan kerjakan tugas-tugas di bawahnya di kertas.

Orientation
Once upon a time, there lived a beautiful girl named Cinderella. She lived with her stepmother and two stepsisters, who always treated her badly. Every day, Cinderella cooked, cleaned, and washed the clothes for her whole family.

Complication
One day, the king invited every young lady in the kingdom to a grand ball, because the prince wanted to choose a wife. Cinderella's stepmother and stepsisters got ready to go, but they did not allow Cinderella to come. Cinderella felt very sad and cried in the garden. Suddenly, a fairy godmother appeared. She waved her magic wand and turned a pumpkin into a beautiful carriage. She also changed Cinderella's old dress into a gorgeous gown and gave her a pair of glass slippers. However, the fairy godmother warned her, "You must leave the ball before midnight, or the magic will disappear."

Complication
At the ball, the prince danced with Cinderella all night, and he fell in love with her. Cinderella was so happy that she almost forgot the warning. When the clock started to strike twelve, she quickly ran down the palace stairs. In her hurry, she dropped one of her glass slippers.

Resolution
The next day, the prince searched the whole kingdom for the owner of the glass slipper. When he arrived at Cinderella's house, the slipper fitted her foot perfectly. The prince recognized her immediately. He married Cinderella, and they lived happily ever after.

JOINT CONSTRUCTION OF TEXT

Text 2 — Pinocchio

Together with your group, read, complete, and analyze the following Pinocchio text. Discuss each part before writing your answers on paper.
Bersama kelompokmu, baca, lengkapi, dan analisis teks Pinocchio berikut. Diskusikan setiap bagian sebelum menuliskan jawaban di kertas.

Orientation
Once upon a time, an old woodcarver named Geppetto (1) ______ (make) a wooden puppet and named it Pinocchio. Geppetto (2) ______ (love) the puppet like his own son. That night, he (3) ______ (wish) upon a star for Pinocchio to become a real boy. Suddenly, a Blue Fairy (4) ______ (appear) and brought the puppet to life. However, she told him that he could only become a real boy if he proved himself brave, truthful, and unselfish.

Complication
On his way to school, Pinocchio (5) ______ (meet) a sly fox and a cat. They (6) ______ (not / tell) him the truth about their plan and tricked him into joining a puppet show instead of studying. Later, whenever Pinocchio (7) ______ (tell) a lie, his nose (8) ______ (grow) longer. One night, he even (9) ______ (go) to an island where naughty boys turned into donkeys, and he almost did not escape.

Resolution
When Pinocchio finally (10) ______ (find out) that Geppetto was trapped inside a giant whale, he (11) ______ (decide) to save him bravely. He (12) ______ (jump) into the sea and rescued his father. Because of his courage and honesty, the Blue Fairy turned him into a real boy at last.

1Complete the text above with the correct Simple Past Tense form of the word in brackets.

💡 Note: number 6 must be in the negative form. Work together with your group and write your answers on paper. 💡 Perhatikan: nomor 6 harus dalam bentuk negative. Kerjakan bersama kelompokmu dan tulis jawaban di kertas.
